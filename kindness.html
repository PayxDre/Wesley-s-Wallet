Lion-Hearted Acts

Wesley had the heart of a lion. Do something kind in his honor, big or small, and tell us about it here. Every act carries a little of him into the world.

The first lion-hearted acts will appear here soon. Yours could be one of them.

Need an idea?

  • Pay for the coffee of the person behind you.
  • Bring a meal to a family with a new baby.
  • Write a thank-you note to a NICU or PICU nurse.
  • Call someone you haven't talked to in a while.
  • Support Wesley's Lion Heart Fund.
