His Story

A short telling, in the words of his family.

Wesley arrived on April 20, 2025, alongside his twin brother Jimmy, two small heartbeats beginning together at the heart of the Prisuta family. From the moment he opened his eyes, he was looking for the people he loved. He found Meghan and Matt, his sisters Vada and Riley, and Jimmy at his side, and he settled into the middle of all of them like he had been waiting his whole life to be there.

He met the world with wide, curious eyes and a smile that came easily. Anyone fortunate enough to spend time with Wesley felt it right away, a wonder for everything around him and an ambition to be part of it. He wanted to play. He wanted to reach for whatever his siblings were reaching for. He wanted to be where the noise was, where the laughter was, where the love was.

He was a son to two parents who adored him, a little brother to two big sisters who watched over him, and a forever twin to Jimmy, the brother who was there from the very first moment. The bond between them was its own kind of language, the kind of closeness that does not need to be explained.

Wesley passed on April 25, 2026, just five days after his first birthday. One year and five days. A short time by some measures, and an entire life of love by every other. He was here. He was deeply loved. And he made everyone who knew him better for having known him.
