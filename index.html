His Story

A short telling, in the words of his family.

Wesley arrived on April 20, 2025, alongside his twin brother Jimmy. Two small heartbeats, beginning together. From his very first days, he carried more than most ever will. He was born with a heart that needed immediate surgery, and he lived with that surgery, and a quiet, remarkable strength, through every day of his short life.

He was a little brother to Vada and Riley, a forever twin to Jimmy, and a son endlessly loved by his parents, Meghan and Matt Prisuta. Anyone fortunate enough to be in Wesley's presence felt it quickly. He met the world with wide-eyed wonder, and an ambition to play that pulled him toward his siblings and family in everything he did. He wanted to be in the middle of it. He wanted to be where the love was.

In March of 2026, an illness brought complications that he and his doctors fought against together. Wesley kept fighting, the way he always had. He passed on April 25, 2026, just five days after his first birthday.

One year and five days. A short time by some measures, and an entire life of love by every other. Wesley was here. He was deeply loved. And he made everyone who knew him better for having known him.
