Help for Families

If your child is in the hospital, or your family has lost a child, you don't have to carry it alone. These are places that can help.

In Wesley's honor

  • Little Lion Family Co. Meghan and Matt's way of showing up for families: Wesley's Lion Heart Fund supports parents with children in the NICU, PICU, and CICU, and Little Lion Village Baskets bring help and essentials to families in crisis or loss.

When your child is in the hospital

  • Ronald McDonald House Charities A place to stay near the hospital, so families can be close to their child.
  • Hand to Hold Peer support for NICU families, from parents who have been there.
  • March of Dimes Information and support for families with babies in the NICU.

After the loss of a child

  • The Compassionate Friends Support groups and resources for families after the death of a child of any age.
  • MISS Foundation Support for families grieving the death of a child.
  • Share Pregnancy & Infant Loss Support Support for families after the loss of a baby.
  • GriefShare Grief support groups that meet in communities across the country.

For brothers and sisters

  • The Dougy Center Resources for grieving children, teens, and the adults who care for them.
  • Comfort Zone Camp Free bereavement camps for children who have lost a parent, sibling, or caregiver.

If you're struggling right now

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline Call or text 988 any time, day or night, to talk with someone.
