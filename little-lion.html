Founded by Wesley's parents, Meghan and Matt, in West Chester, PA.

Building a village that shows up. In honor of Wesley.

Little Lion. Big Help.

In Wesley's honor, Little Lion gives back to families in the hardest seasons: a child in critical care, the loss of a child or parent, and the early days after a new baby.

Inspired by the village that carried us, I hope to carry Wesley's love with us into this new chapter, by showing up for families who need it most.
